100% Financial Transparency

How Your Donations Are Allocated

We ensure every dollar contributed goes directly to medical consumables, surgeon fees, and free patient relief.

70%

Surgical Supplies & IOL Lenses

Covers American Alcon/German Carl Zeiss hydrophobic foldable intraocular lenses, surgical viscoelastics, micro-blades, and sterile OT gear.

15%

Free Medicines & Glasses

Provides post-operative antibiotic drops, anti-inflammatory medications, protective dark glasses, and free reading spectacles for patients.

10%

Rural Patient Logistics

Funds transport ambulances to move elderly patients safely from remote river islands (Chars) to hospital OT centers and back home.

5%

Follow-up & Monitoring

Covers 1st week, 1st month, and 3rd month post-op checkups to guarantee 100% infection-free recovery and vision restoration.
