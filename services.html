Chokher Alo Retina Glaucoma Hospital & Phaco Center

Comprehensive Eye Care &
Advanced Ophthalmic Treatments

All kinds of eye-related problems are treated at Chokher Alo Retina Glaucoma Hospital & Phaco Center (Nizam-Hasina Foundation Hospital) using state-of-the-art German Carl Zeiss & Topcon microscopes, Alcon Centurion Phaco systems, and digital diagnostic suites.

Phaco Microsurgery

1. Cataract Surgery (5-Minute Phaco)

Cataract Surgery performed using world-famous American Alcon Centurion® and AMO Phaco machines equipped with German Carl Zeiss & Topcon High Quality microscopes. Precision sutureless surgery completed within 5 minutes.

5-Min Sutureless
Tear Duct Surgery

2. DCR & DCT Operation (Watering Eye)

A watery eye can be due to many causes, essentially stemming from an imbalance between tear production by the tear gland and drainage blockage. DCR (Dacryocystorhinostomy) and DCT surgeries bypass blocked nasolacrimal ducts to restore normal drainage.

Nasolacrimal Bypass
Corneal & Eyelid Microsurgery

3. Pterygium & Chalazion Surgery

The pterygium tissue is meticulously excised and the bare scleral area is covered with an autograft extracted from the patient’s own conjunctiva to prevent recurrence. Chalazion eyelid cysts are also safely drained and removed.

Conjunctival Autograft
Glaucoma Specialty

4. Glaucoma Operation & Treatment

While damage caused by glaucoma cannot be reversed, early treatment, laser iridotomy, trabeculectomy filtering operations, and regular eye pressure checkups slow down or prevent further permanent optic nerve vision loss.

Optic Nerve Defense
Advanced Laser Unit

5. YAG Laser & Green Laser

Nd:YAG lasers optically pumped using laser diodes provide painless posterior capsulotomy after cataract surgery and peripheral iridotomy. Green laser photocoagulation treats retinal tears, diabetic leakage, and macular edema.

Non-Invasive Laser
Vitreo-Retina Care

6. Specialized Retina Treatment

The retina is the innermost, light-sensitive layer of neural tissue containing photoreceptor cells (rods and cones). We provide advanced treatment for retinal detachment, macular degeneration, and vascular occlusions.

Photoreceptor Protection
Anti-VEGF Injection Therapy

7. Intra-Vitreal Avastin Injection

Intravitreal drug delivery directly into the vitreous cavity is a highly effective treatment for severe retinal conditions, including Age-Related Macular Degeneration (AMD), Diabetic Retinopathy, and Retinal Vein Occlusions (RVO).

Sterile Injection Suite
Digital Retinal Imaging

8. Color Fundus Photography & OCT

High-definition Fundus photography provides color and red-free digital images of the retina. Combined with Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT), it captures micron-level cross-sectional scans of retinal nerve layers.

Micron HD Imaging
Pediatric Ophthalmology

9. Child Cataract & Probing Operation

Specialized pediatric care addressing lacrimal drainage structures and congenital tear duct blockages. Gentle lacrimal probing opens blocked nasolacrimal grooves, while pediatric cataract surgery preserves early vision development.

Specialized Child Care
Ocular Muscle Surgery

10. Squint Surgery (Strabismus Correction)

Squint (Strabismus) occurs when both eyes are misaligned. Contrary to common myths, squint is NOT permanent and can be fully corrected through extraocular muscle alignment microsurgery for both children and adults.

Alignment Corrected
Ophthalmic Ultrasound Suite

11. Biometry, Pachymetry, A-Scan & B-Scan

High-frequency diagnostic ultrasound safely probes the delicate internal structures of the eye without damage. Provides precise IOL power calculations (Biometry), corneal thickness measurement (Pachymetry), and posterior segment B-Scans.

Precise IOL & Ultrasound
Diagnostic Lab & Cardiac

12. Pathological Lab & ECG Services

In-house pathology lab for routine pre-operative blood screening, HbA1c diabetic monitoring, and Electrocardiogram (ECG) testing to evaluate cardiac health and electrical activity prior to ocular surgeries.

Complete Pre-Op Fitness
Diabetic & Vascular Clinic

13. Diabetic & Hypertensive Retinopathy

Diabetic and Hypertensive retinopathy are leading causes of visual impairment and blindness. Our specialized clinic provides comprehensive fundus evaluations, angiography, laser sealing, and strict glycemic monitoring protocols.

Retinal Bleeding Control
24/7 Urgent Sight Care

14. 24/7 Eye Emergency & Trauma Casualty

Our specialized eye casualty department handles urgent sight threats, ocular chemical burns, corneal lacerations, foreign body removal, and acute glaucoma spikes on a 24/7 clinical priority basis.

Open 24 Hours / 7 Days Emergency Call
