About Us

World-Class Eye Care Right at Your Fingertips

Chokher Alo Retina Glaucoma Hospital & Phaco Center was established with a singular mission—to deliver top-tier, affordable eye care to people across all walks of life in Bangladesh.

Under the supervision of fellowship-trained consultants from National Institute of Ophthalmology & Hospital (NIO&H), BIRDEM, and prestigious institutes in the UK and India, we provide definitive solutions for diabetic retinopathy, glaucoma, complex cataracts, and refractive errors.
